# Intake interview

Use AskUserQuestion. Check the vault client folder and PRODUCT.md first; ask only what is missing. Three or four questions per round, at most three rounds.

## Round 1: the client and the job
- Client name, business, and what they do.
- Who the site is for (their customers) and what a visitor should do.
- Pages needed (for example home, about, services, contact) and any special functions (booking, forms, shop).

## Round 2: content on hand
- Is the copy written? If yes, where is it. If no, who is providing it. Do not write marketing copy to fill the gap; list it as an open item.
- Logo, brand colors, fonts, existing site.
- Real photos, testimonials, results, case studies, pricing that exist and may be used. State absences so nothing is fabricated.

## Round 3: look and tools
- Reference sites the client or user likes or dislikes.
- Visual media: video or animated hero with Flow Motion Director (ask for the concept in the user's own words), stock or generated stills, or none.
- Stack: ask per client. Offer static HTML/CSS, Astro, Next.js, or "you choose".
- Deadline, hosting, domain, and whether Framer Motion (React sites) or GSAP is preferred for motion.

## After the interview
1. Write `Clients/<client-name>/Brief.md` in the vault with the answers and an Open items list.
2. Run `impeccable init`; its PRODUCT.md must reflect the brief.
3. Move to Phase 1.
